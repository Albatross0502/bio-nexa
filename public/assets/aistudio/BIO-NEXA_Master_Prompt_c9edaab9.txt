     10 enrollments             26 external_sources
     11 quizzes                 27 sync_logs
     12 quiz_questions          28 reports
     13 quiz_results            29 contact_messages
     14 certificates            30 newsletter_subscribers
     15 exams                   31 audit_logs
     16 exam_resources          32 site_settings
10.2 EVERY TABLE: primary key, created_at, updated_at, foreign
     keys, indexes on search/filter columns, CHECK constraints.
10.3 users: id, name, email (unique), password_hash, role
     (student/company/admin), email_verified, status, timestamps.
     Never store plain passwords.
10.4 SHARED COLUMNS for jobs, internships, scholarships,
     research_papers, news_articles:
       source_id, source_url, external_id, status
       (draft/pending/published/expired/rejected),
       last_synced_at, expires_at, content_hash,
       UNIQUE(source_id, external_id)
10.5 Use lookup tables or CHECK enums for job type, experience
     level, qualification, category.
10.6 SEARCH: use SQLite FTS5 virtual tables for jobs, courses,
     scholarships, research.
10.7 Provide numbered SQL migrations (001_, 002_ ...), a migrate
     runner, and seed.js.
10.8 ACCESS RULES (enforced in server code, tested):
     - Public sees only status = published.
     - Students read/write only their own data.
     - Companies manage only their own listings and see only
       their own applicants.
     - Admin manages everything.
     - audit_logs: app writes, only admin reads.
